## Why this niche
- Money and economics explainers sit in one of YouTube's highest-paying ad categories. Common public estimates put RPM around $8–20 for a US audience. Treat that as an estimate, never a promise.
- $5,000 a month at an RPM of $10 needs about 500,000 long-form views a month.
- The topics are evergreen and animation-friendly: charts, flows of money, maps and simple characters.

## Format
- **Long-form video:** 8–10 minutes, 16:9 1920×1080, animated explainer, English narration. Two a week.
- **Shorts:** three a week, each cut from a long video's strongest 30–45 seconds, ending with a pointer to the full video.

## Quality rules (zero mistakes)
1. Every number and claim has a reliable source (central banks, government statistics, company filings, major outlets). Store the sources in the episode's `meta.json`. An unsourced claim gets cut.
2. Explain how things work. **Never give personal financial or investment advice**, and say so in the description.
3. Make every video original: our own script, structure and visuals. Never copy or reword another channel's video. Variety protects monetization under YouTube's inauthentic-content policy.
4. Declare synthetic voice / AI assistance where YouTube requires it. Animation is not "realistic altered content", so leave the AI flag off unless realistic people or places are generated.
5. Check the full video visually before publishing: readable text, no overlaps, audio in sync, correct spelling.
6. Packaging: the title and thumbnail are one unit (use the `yt-package` rules). Titles carry a number or a name, and the thumbnail uses at most 3 words.
